Understanding Tonicity

Visual guides for the three main states.

Isotonic

↔️

Equal solute concentration inside and outside. No net movement.

Hypertonic

➡️ ⬅️

High solute concentration outside. Water moves out of the cell.

Hypotonic

⬅️ ➡️

Low solute concentration outside. Water moves into the cell.
